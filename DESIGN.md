# design.md

# Dovetail Inspired Landing Page

> A premium dark-mode SaaS landing page focused on clarity, depth, and product storytelling.

---

# Design Philosophy

The page should feel like professional software rather than a marketing website.

Every section exists to communicate trust, intelligence, and product capability.

The visual language is minimal, technical, and restrained.

There is almost no decorative color except a subtle orange accent.

Everything is built around whitespace, grid alignment, thin strokes, and layered depth.

---

# Overall Mood

- Premium
- Enterprise
- Technical
- Minimal
- Calm
- Confident
- High-end software
